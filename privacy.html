2. Visiting this website

This website is hosted exclusively on Cloudflare Pages, a service of Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you open a page, your browser automatically sends technical data to Cloudflare's servers. This can include:

  • Your IP address
  • Date and time of your visit
  • The page you open and the website you came from
  • Your browser and operating system

This data is needed to deliver the website to you and to keep it secure and stable. It is not used to identify you and is not combined with other data. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in running a secure website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

We have concluded a data processing agreement with Cloudflare. Cloudflare may process data in other countries, including the USA, and is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. More information: Cloudflare privacy policy.

All fonts, images and scripts are loaded from this website itself. No data is sent to other services such as Google when you visit the website.

3. Cookies and tracking

This website does not use cookies, tracking tools, analytics or advertising services, and no other third-party services are used.

4. Contact via WhatsApp and Instagram

The WhatsApp and Instagram buttons on this website are simple links. No data is sent to WhatsApp or Instagram until you click one of them. After that, the privacy policies of these services apply: WhatsApp and Instagram (both run by Meta).

If you send Jos a message, he uses the information you share, for example your name, phone number, travel dates and group size, only to answer your request and to organize your tour. It is only shared with others when this is needed for your tour, for example with a partner guide or for transport you asked Jos to arrange.
