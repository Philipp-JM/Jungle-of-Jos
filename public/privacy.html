2. Visiting this website

This website is hosted exclusively on Cloudflare Pages, a service of Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you open a page, your browser automatically sends technical data to Cloudflare's servers. This can include:

  • Your IP address
  • Date and time of your visit
  • The page you open and the website you came from
  • Your browser and operating system

This data is needed to deliver the website to you and to keep it secure and stable. It is not used to identify you and is not combined with other data. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in running a secure website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Jos has no access to these technical server logs. Cloudflare stores them only for a short time for operation and security, according to its own deletion periods.

We have concluded a data processing agreement with Cloudflare. Cloudflare may process data in other countries, including the USA, and is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. More information: Cloudflare privacy policy.

All fonts and images are loaded from this website itself. Apart from Cloudflare (hosting and the visitor statistics described in section 3), no other services are used. No data is sent to services such as Google or Meta when you visit the website.

3. Visitor statistics (Cloudflare Web Analytics)

To see how many people visit the website and which pages are read most, we use Cloudflare Web Analytics, a service of our hosting provider Cloudflare (see section 2). When a page loads, a small script from Cloudflare sends anonymous usage data to Cloudflare, for example the page you open, the website you came from, your browser, device type and country, and how fast the page loaded.

Cloudflare Web Analytics works differently from tools such as Google Analytics or Matomo:

  • It does not set cookies and does not store anything on your device (no local storage)
  • It does not use tracking pixels or advertising IDs
  • It does not create a "fingerprint" of your device from your IP address, browser or other data
  • It does not follow you across other websites or build a profile of you

We only see summarized numbers, for example visits per page or per country. We cannot identify individual visitors. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in understanding which information on the website is useful and improving it (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). You can object to this at any time (see section 7), or block the script with a content blocker in your browser. More information: Cloudflare Web Analytics.

4. Contact via WhatsApp and Instagram

The WhatsApp and Instagram buttons on this website are simple links. No data is sent to WhatsApp or Instagram until you click one of them. After that, the privacy policies of these services apply: WhatsApp and Instagram (both run by Meta).

If you send Jos a message, he uses the information you share, for example your name, phone number, travel dates and group size, only to answer your request and to organize your tour. The legal basis is the preparation and fulfilment of your booking (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Your data is not sold or used for advertising. It is only shared with others when this is needed for your tour, for example with a partner guide or for transport you asked Jos to arrange.

Jos lives and works in Indonesia, so your messages are received and processed there. Indonesia is a country outside the EU for which there is no EU adequacy decision, so the level of data protection may differ from the EU. This transfer is necessary to answer your request and to organize your tour (Art. 49(1)(b) GDPR).

There is no online booking and no online payment. Tours are paid in cash on site, so no bank or card details are processed. Jos keeps your messages only as long as he needs them to answer your request and to organize your tour. You can ask him to delete them at any time.
